Markets

Prediction pools on the apps in the Solana dApp Store: Jupiter's volume, Phantom's revenue, Jito's staked SOL, the pump.fun app, ORE, and the Seeker itself. Pick a range and stake SKR; winners split the losing pools. The fee is 3% of winnings only, less if you bet early, hold a Seeker Genesis Token or mine ORE. Every result is backed by hourly snapshots whose hashes are on-chain.
